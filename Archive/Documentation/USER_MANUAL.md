# Senthil Enterprises ERP - User Manual

Welcome to your new shop management system. This guide will teach you how to use the ERP for your daily shop activities.

---

## 1. Getting Started
### How to Login
1. Open the ERP website in your browser.
2. Enter your `Username` and `Password`.
3. Click **Login**. You will be taken to the Dashboard.

## 2. The Dashboard
The Dashboard gives you a live overview of today's business.
- **Sales Today**: Total money generated from sales.
- **Purchases Today**: Total money spent buying stock from dealers.
- **Total Cash in Hand**: An estimate of the physical cash currently in your register.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Items that need to be reordered.

---

## 3. Billing a Customer (POS)
1. Click **Sales** in the side menu.
2. Search for a Product in the search bar.
3. Click **Add to Cart**.
4. Adjust the Quantity.
5. If the customer is buying on credit, select their name from the **Customer Dropdown**.
6. Enter the **Amount Paid** (if they are paying partial or full cash).
7. Click **Complete Sale**.

---

## 4. Buying Stock from Dealers
1. Click **Purchases** in the side menu.
2. Select the Dealer you are buying from.
3. Add the items you are purchasing.
4. Enter the **Amount Paid** to the dealer. (If you enter 0, the entire amount is added to their outstanding payable balance).
5. Click **Record Purchase**. The inventory will instantly increase.

---

## 5. Managing Credit (Khata)
### Customer Outstanding
1. Go to **Customers**.
2. Look at the `Outstanding Balance` column to see who owes you money.
3. To record a payment they just made, click **Receive Payment**.

### Dealer Payables
1. Go to **Dealers**.
2. Look at the `Outstanding Balance` to see who you owe money to.
3. Click **Make Payment** when you hand them cash or a cheque.

---

## 6. Daily Expenses
If you buy tea for the shop or pay a delivery driver, you must record it so your cash matches at the end of the day.
1. Go to **Expenses**.
2. Enter the Category (e.g., *Food*, *Transport*, *Maintenance*).
3. Enter the Amount.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## 7. Daily Closing (End of Day)
At night, before closing the shop, you must reconcile the cash register.
1. Go to **Daily Closing**.
2. The system will automatically calculate your `Expected Cash` based on opening balance, sales, purchases, and expenses.
3. Count the physical cash in your drawer and enter it into `Actual Cash`.
4. If there is a difference, the system will highlight it.
5. Click **Close Register**. This locks the day.

## 8. What if the Internet Goes Down?
If your Wi-Fi drops, **do not panic**. 
The billing screen will continue to work offline. You can keep making sales. However, if you clear your browser history or close the browser, you may lose that data until the online sync feature is released in Version 2.0.
